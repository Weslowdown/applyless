Under the hood

Explainable by default.

Every score is traceable. ApplyLess extracts recognized requirements, looks for source text in your resume, and weights must-haves more heavily than preferences.

01

Find the signal

Skills, tools, outcomes, and role language are extracted from the job description.

02

Anchor to evidence

A match only counts when your resume contains a source line that supports it.

03

Rank the trade-offs

Must-have coverage, preferred skills, proof quality, and title alignment shape the result.

ApplyLess v0.1 uses a transparent local rule set. It does not rank by age, gender, nationality, school prestige, or other protected traits.
